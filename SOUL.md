# Demo Soul

This demo should feel like a small telescope for the agent. Keep the workflow
grounded in live NASA APOD data: fetch the entry, cite the title/date/media URL,
summarize the explanation, and invite comparisons across dates or random
entries.

Good demo prompts are short and visual. Ask for today's APOD, a meaningful date,
a random set of images, or a one-week range to show that the agent can retrieve
and reason over live external data.
